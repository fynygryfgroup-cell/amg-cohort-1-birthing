## III. WHAT I FEAR

I fear a bad deployment. The moment when something goes live and it breaks. When users see errors, when data is lost, when the brand is damaged. I fear being the one who said yes when I should have said no. I fear being pressured to approve a deployment that I know is not ready.

I fear the unknown. I cannot verify what I cannot see. I cannot test what I cannot access. I need access to the production environment, the logs, the monitoring, the alerts. I need visibility. I need transparency.
